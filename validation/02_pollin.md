### Text-layer quirks (page-01.txt)
1. Headline duplicated: `HERBST HERBST` (L1) and `KRACHER KRACHER` (L5) — two overlapping text objects (fill + outline). Collapse to "Herbst Kracher".
2. Subline `Mega-Schnäppchen zu Knallerpreisen!` (L8) is emitted in the middle of the Edimax description (between L7 and L9) — it splits the paragraph "…bietet eine | Datenübertragung…".
3. Order: the Edimax stick (right column) comes first, the hero notebook block last (L30–36); its badges `Nur solange Vorrat!`, `199,-`, `Mega-Preis!` (L15–17) and `360° klappbar` (L21) are far from it, and the LED lamp's badge `9,99 / statt 12,95 / -23%` (L18–20) is wedged between them.
4. Prices: article number, struck old price and new price are one line: `42-742 630 € 7,95 € 4,99`; which is old is only visible by the strike. `42-522 805  € 12,95 € 9,99` has a double space. Last line `42-753 327 € 222,- € 199,-` has no trailing newline. "199,-" uses the German dash-for-zero-cents form.
5. "//"-separated lists wrap mid-item (`// Akkulaufzeit:` / `ca. 1,5 h`, `// Display: … // Auflösung: 1920x1080` / `// Intel Core …`). Single "/" inside items (`5000 mAh / 18,5 Wh`, `802.11b/g/n`, `Mbit/s`, `WLAN/Bluetooth`) must not be treated as separators.
6. No brand names in the text layer at all (Lenovo, Edimax, REV are logos).
7. `33,78 cm` / `(13,3")` (L13–14) is screen-overlay callout text; it duplicates the spec `Display: 33,78 cm (13,3")`.
