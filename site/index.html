How it works

  1. You send the PDF you already publish.
  2. We convert it into a web page with real headings, lists and tables, and check every price against the PDF.
  3. You review it and put the link where the PDF link is today. Anything we could not check is marked for you.

What you get

  • An accessible web page: a link to share, code to embed in your site, or the files to host yourself.
  • A short report of what was checked automatically and what needs your eyes.
  • When your menu changes, send the new PDF: the same link shows the new version.
  • Your original PDF stays as it is.

We do not certify legal compliance. The report says exactly what was checked, so you can decide.
