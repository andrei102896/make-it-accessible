# Sections in columns

**Seen in:** 16 of 19 US menus print sections side by side (Carmine's 3 columns in red frames, Almond and Pastabilities 4, Sarge's 3–4); Gargiulo's mixes full-width rows with two-column groups; Prohibition Post flows one article across 4 columns.

**Rule (2026-10-16, `html/layout.ts`):** a section's column is its heading's position; its items' flow decides its shape. Narrow sections cluster by heading overlap into a grid of ≤ 4 columns (desktop) and stack in print order (phone); a section whose items flow across > 60% of the page is a full-width row (two-column list inside); a flowing row to the right of one narrow column stands beside it (side band). A list whose items are stacked and whose median item spans at least half the page prints one dish per line (`wide`, `html/shapes.ts`, 2026-10-16): one column everywhere, and when most descriptions start on the name's line (`run-on`: Noah's, Almond, Moosewood, Ta-boo, Nick & Toni's, Bernini) the name, description and price share the line, the lead description before the price; otherwise (Jack's, Ridgway) rows are capped at 44rem. A `wide` list is a full row in the bands too, whatever its names and prices span (Moosewood's salads next to the dressings note). On a menu, a two-column table without headers whose second column is only prices (Carmine's beers) renders as menu rows.
