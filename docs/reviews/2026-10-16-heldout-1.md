# Held-out batch 1 — first-time quality on unseen menus (2026-10-16)

Why: Andrei, 2026-10-16: every PDF is a scenario to learn from, so the next one comes out right the first time; otherwise each new PDF is work from zero. Until now every layout round was judged on the same 19 menus we tune on. This batch measures what the engine does on menus it has never seen, run once with no change in between.

## Setup
- 10 menus never processed before: 8 independent restaurants in New York and Florida from the NY/FL list (Claudio's, Gigi Trattoria, Michael's Genuine, Hogfish, Boater's Grill, Lexington Candy Shop, 2nd Ave Deli, La Segunda Bakery) and 2 from the US list to reach ten (FIG, Kenny & Ziggy's). PDFs local in `validation/testdocs/heldout/`.
- `pnpm adhoc` (Sonnet 5.5, medium), then `pnpm check:page` and `pnpm review:page` on each. Engine at e1463a8.
- Cost: $2.35 conversion (25 pages), $0.22 reviewer.

## Patterns, by how many menus they reach
1. **The name is artwork we do not ingest (8 of 9).** The logo is drawn as vector paths in 6 (Claudio's, Michael's, Boater's, Lexington, FIG, Kenny & Ziggy's: 0 raster images on page 1 per pdftohtml, 163–1,171 drawn paths), so no figure exists to crop; 4 carry pictures the SVG shows but pdftohtml does not report (Michael's, Lexington, FIG, Kenny & Ziggy's). Same root as the frames: a layer of the PDF we do not read (artwork: logos, banners, line art, seals).
2. **Display and script lettering rendered plain (7 of 9).** Brush script (Claudio's), hand-drawn condensed (Kenny & Ziggy's), italic script (Lexington, Gigi, 2nd Ave), outlined shadowed caps (La Segunda), slab (Michael's). Part is text in faces the library lacks, part is lettering drawn as artwork (pattern 1).
3. **Page colour, banners and background art (6 of 9).** Kenny & Ziggy's red page, Boater's banners and cream wavy panel (a curved fill, not a rectangle), Claudio's line art, Gigi's olive banner, 2nd Ave's mosaic border, La Segunda's ribbons.
4. **Printed arrangement not kept (6 of 9).** Multi-column sheets stacked (Lexington, 2nd Ave), a cover beside content (La Segunda, Gigi), free arrangements (Michael's, Boater's).
5. **Text layer faults reach the content (3 of 9), our wedge.** A lost "ti" ligature ("fes ve" for "festive") makes 68 of Gigi's 75 errors; cited words cut short ("Pu" for "Purchase", Lexington); price forms read as no amount: "$30/dz" (Gigi), "3.99/4.99/5.99" (La Segunda).
6. **Section chips list things that are not sections (4 of 9):** a notice ("If we are out of something…"), the restaurant's name, an order form title.
7. **Bugs:** a brand colour left below 4.5:1 (Kenny & Ziggy's, axe color-contrast at every width); a copy-protected PDF stops the pipeline unnamed (Hogfish).

## Step 1 done the same evening (after two critic rounds)
Patterns 5–7 fixed as patterns, each with a fixture from the menu that showed it: lost ligatures filled where the gap shows them (Gigi 31 words, Noah's 10 on the tuned set); sizes and prices per unit shown as printed, unlabelled sizes in review, dates and cut counts still errors (Gigi 43, La Segunda 3); our secondary grey made readable on a light panel (Kenny & Ziggy's); a copy-protected PDF refused by name; a page title or a sentence is no section chip, a menu cover has none (Kenny & Ziggy's, Gigi, 2nd Ave; on the tuned set Mezzaluna, Nick & Toni's, Rucola and Prohibition gained their real sections). Validator errors on the held-out set: Gigi 75 → 1 ("Ord Online!", correct), La Segunda 3 → 0 (3 in review), Lexington 5 (text drawn as artwork: pattern 1). check:page clean on all 33 documents. These menus are now seen: the next held-out batch measures the change.

## What this changes
The tuned set hid patterns 1 and 5: its logos happened to be raster images and its text layers clean. Each pattern above is a layer or a form, not a menu; each gets a fixture from this batch when fixed, and the next held-out batch (different menus) measures it again.
